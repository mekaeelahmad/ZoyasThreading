What we offer

Threading removes hair with a twisted cotton thread for sharp definition and less irritation on sensitive skin. Popular for face shaping and maintenance between appointments.

Eyebrow threading

Certified shaping and cleanup to define your arch, thickness, and symmetry—tailored to your face.

Full face

Cheeks, jawline, forehead, and upper lip for an all-over smooth, photo-ready finish.

Sideburns & neck

Clean edges along the hairline and sideburns for a polished look that grows in softly.

Upper lip & chin

Quick, precise hair removal for common areas—ideal for sensitive skin and detailed lines.

Combo & custom

Mix brows with lip, chin, or full face in one visit. Ask what combination works best for you.

First-time friendly

New to threading? We'll explain the process and go at your pace so you leave confident and comfortable.

Why choose threading

Threading plucks hair at the follicle without heat or harsh chemicals, so many clients prefer it for delicate facial areas. With certified brow expertise, every session focuses on balance, proportion, and a look that fits your style—not a one-size stencil.

Certified expertise

Holding certification in eyebrow threading means training in technique, sanitation, and shaping principles—so you get consistent, professional care in a calm setting.
